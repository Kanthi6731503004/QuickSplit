Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Overview

QuickSplit ("the App") is a bill-splitting calculator. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data Collection

QuickSplit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data (bills, people, items) is stored locally on your device using an on-device database.

No Account Required

QuickSplit does not require registration, login, or any personal information to use.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with analytics, advertising, or tracking services. When you use the share feature (WhatsApp, LINE, etc.), data is shared through your device's native sharing mechanism — QuickSplit does not access or store this data.

Data Storage

All bill data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will delete all stored data. The web version stores data in your browser's local storage.

Children's Privacy

QuickSplit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at quicksplit888@gmail.com.
